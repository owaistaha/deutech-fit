## Main post

```
"English-speaking team." 
…then the interview is in German. 🇩🇪

If you're an international student applying for jobs in Germany, you know this one.

The job ad is in English, but somewhere in paragraph six it says "gute Deutschkenntnisse".
Or it's a full-time role you legally can't take on a student visa.
Or your CV says "Intern" while German recruiters search for "Werkstudent".

So I built DeuTech Fit, a free Chrome extension for LinkedIn and StepStone.

Open a job, click one button, and in a few seconds it tells you:

🔎 The German level the employer REALLY expects (A1–C2), even in English ads
🎓 Whether the job fits your visa: the 20 h/week student rule, Werkstudent, Blue Card, Chancenkarte
📄 The German job title and the CV keywords you're missing for German ATS filters

A few things I cared about:
✅ Your CV is read on your device. The PDF is never uploaded.
✅ No account, no tracking, no ads
✅ Free. It runs on Google Gemini with your own free API key.

I built it with React, TypeScript and Plasmo, using Gemini for the analysis.

If you're job hunting in Germany, try it and tell me what it gets wrong. Feedback from real job ads is what makes it better. 🙏

🔗 Link in the first comment.

#Germany #Werkstudent #InternationalStudents #JobSearch #ChromeExtension
```
