```
DeuTech Fit: Germany Job Checker – German Level, Visa & Werkstudent
```

terms (Germany job, German level, visa, Werkstudent) and avoids the "LinkedIn" trademark in
the title, which reviewers sometimes flag. "LinkedIn" and "StepStone" go in the summary and
description, where using them descriptively is fine.

## 5. Detailed description (English, plain text, paste as-is)

```
Applying for jobs in Germany as an international student? DeuTech Fit reads the job ad you're looking at on LinkedIn or StepStone, compares it with your CV, and tells you in seconds what the posting doesn't say out loud.

The ad is in English. Do they secretly expect German? Can you even take this job on a student visa? What should your Lebenslauf say so German recruiters and ATS systems find you? DeuTech Fit answers all three.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT IT CHECKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🇩🇪 HIDDEN GERMAN LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS
• Detects the German level (A1–C2) the employer really expects, even in English-language job ads
• Understands recruiter phrases like "verhandlungssicher", "fließend Deutsch", "gute Deutschkenntnisse" and "German is a plus"
• Spots indirect signals: client-facing roles in DACH, German-only benefits text, Mittelstand and public-sector employers
• Warns you when the job needs more than your level: "⚠️ Buried B2 German Requirement Found"

🎓 VISA & WERKSTUDENT COMPATIBILITY
• Checks hours and contract type against the 20 hours/week student rule (§ 16b AufenthG)
• Flags whether the role is Werkstudent / working-student compatible
• Considers the EU Blue Card salary thresholds, the Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte), post-study job seeker permits and more
• Gives a clear HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW visa-fit rating with the reason

📄 GERMAN CV (LEBENSLAUF) OPTIMISATION
• Recommends the German job title recruiters search for, e.g. "Intern" → "Werkstudent Softwareentwicklung (m/w/d)"
• Lists up to 10 German keywords from the ad that are missing from your CV, ready to copy into your Lebenslauf
• Helps your application get through German ATS filters

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Add your free Google Gemini API key, your German level, your residence permit and your CV (PDF) in Settings
2. Open any job on LinkedIn Jobs or StepStone.de
3. Click the DeuTech Fit badge → "Analyze Job Fit for Germany"
4. Get your language, visa and CV results in a floating panel or in Chrome's side panel

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIVACY FIRST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Your CV is parsed on your own device. The PDF is never uploaded anywhere.
• Your settings and CV text are stored only in your browser
• No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads
• Job text and CV text are sent only to Google's Gemini API, with your own key, when you click Analyze

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO IT'S FOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

International students, graduates and tech professionals looking for jobs in Germany: Werkstudent jobs, internships (Praktikum), junior developer, data, IT and engineering roles in Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt and across Germany.

Works with: LinkedIn Jobs (including the new job search layout) and StepStone.de.

DeuTech Fit is free. If it helps your job search, you can support development with the "Buy me a coffee" button inside the extension. This is optional and unlocks nothing.

Note: visa results are AI-generated guidance based on public German residence rules, not legal advice. Always confirm with your Ausländerbehörde or your university's international office.

DeuTech Fit is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn, StepStone or Google.
```

## 9. Single purpose (Privacy practices tab)

```
DeuTech Fit analyzes the job posting the user is viewing on LinkedIn or StepStone against the user's own CV and profile, to show the expected German language level, visa/Werkstudent compatibility and German CV keyword suggestions.
```

## 10. Permission justifications (Privacy practices tab)

**storage**
```
Stores the user's settings (German level, residence permit type, target role), the text extracted from their CV, their own Gemini API key and the most recent analysis result locally in chrome.storage.local. Nothing is synced or sent to the developer.
```

**sidePanel**
```
Shows the analysis results for the current job posting in Chrome's side panel, opened when the user clicks the toolbar icon.
```

**Host permission: https://www.linkedin.com/***
```
A content script reads the job posting the user is currently viewing (title, company, location, description) and shows the analysis panel. It runs on all linkedin.com paths because LinkedIn is a single-page app: users reach job pages by in-app navigation from other LinkedIn pages, which never triggers a page load on /jobs/*. The panel only appears on /jobs pages. The extension fetches the public posting for the selected job ID only when the user clicks "Analyze".
```

**Host permission: https://www.stepstone.de/***
```
A content script reads the job posting the user is currently viewing on StepStone and shows the analysis panel. Data is only read when the user clicks "Analyze".
```

**Host permission: https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/***
```
Sends the job posting text and the user's CV text to Google's Gemini API, using the user's own API key, to generate the analysis. This is the extension's core feature and happens only when the user clicks "Analyze".
```

**Remote code**
```
No, I am not using remote code. All JavaScript is bundled in the package. The Gemini API returns JSON data only, and it is never executed.
```

## 11. Data usage disclosures (Privacy practices tab)

Donations: the "Buy me a coffee" button is a plain link to buymeacoffee.com. It is not an in-app
purchase, so leave the dashboard's "in-app purchases" option off.

Tick these data types:
- ☑ **Personally identifiable information:** the user's CV text may contain their name, email, address and so on
- ☑ **Website content:** text of the job posting being analyzed

Leave everything else unticked (health, financial, authentication info, personal communications,
location, web history, user activity).

Tick all three certifications:
- ☑ I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases
- ☑ I do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to my item's single purpose
- ☑ I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes
